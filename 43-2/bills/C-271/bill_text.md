# Bill C-271: An Act to amend the Governor General's Act

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Governor General's Act` to set the Governor General's annual salary at $1. It also repeals Part II of the Act in order to remove the right to a retiring annuity and amends other acts in consequence.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Governor General's Act

### Section 1

Subsection 4(1) of the `Governor General's Act` is replaced by the following:

### Section 4

  **(1)** There shall be payable to the Governor General **an annual** salary of **$1** beginning on **February 1, 2021**.

### Section 2

Section 4.1 of the Act is repealed.

### Section 3

Part II of the Act is repealed.


## Consequential amendments


### Garnishment, Attachment and Pension Diversion Act

### Section 4

The schedule to the `Garnishment, Attachment and Pension Diversion Act` is amended by striking out the reference to "1 `Governor General's Act`.".


### Pension Benefits Division Act

### Section 5

The definition pension plan in section 2 of the `Pension Benefits Division Act` is amended by striking out subparagraph (a)(viii).


### Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act

### Section 6

Paragraph 8(2)(c) of the `Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act` is replaced by the following:

any benefit calculated by reference to an annuity payable under the `Judges Act`,

### Section 7

Schedule I to the Act is amended by striking out the reference to "1 `Governor General's Act`.".


## Transitional provision

### Section 8

Part II of the `Governor General's Act`, as it read immediately before February 1, 2021, continues to apply with respect to an annuity referred to in section 6 or 7 that became payable before that date.


## Coming into Force

### Section 9

This Act is deemed to have come into force on February 1, 2021.
