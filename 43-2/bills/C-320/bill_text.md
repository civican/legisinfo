# Bill C-320: An Act to amend the Textile Labelling Act (animal skin, hair and fur)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Textile Labelling Act` to modify requirements in respect of the skin, hair and fur of certain animal species.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Textile Labelling Act

### Section 1

The definition **textile fibre** in section 2 of the `Textile Labelling Act` is replaced by the following:

**textile fibre** means any natural or manufactured matter that is capable of being made into a yarn or fabric and includes human hair, kapok, feathers and down and **animal skin, as well as** animal hair **and** fur, **whether or not they have** been removed from **the** animal skin; (**fibre textile**)

### Section 2

Section 6 of the Act is renumbered as subsection 6(1) and is amended by adding the following:

**(2)** Despite subparagraph (1)(b)(i), in the case of an article that contains *felis catus* or *canis lupus familiaris* skin or hair or fur not removed from the skin of those species, the label shall show the generic name of that textile fibre, regardless of the percentage of the total fibre mass of the article that it comprises.
