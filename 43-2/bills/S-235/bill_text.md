# Bill S-235: An Act to amend the Canadian Victims Bill of Rights (posting of information about the victim)

**Sponsor**: Senator Boisvenu

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canadian Victims Bill of Rights` by adding a right relating to the posting, by the offender, of information about the victim.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Short Title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the *Véronique Barbe Act*.


## `Canadian Victims Bill of Rights`

### Section 2

The `Canadian Victims Bill of Rights` is amended by adding the following after section 11:

### Section 11.1

Every victim has the right to have reasonable and necessary measures taken by the appropriate authorities in the criminal justice system to prohibit the offender from posting any information about the victim on the Internet.
