# Bill C-287: An Act to amend the Official Development Assistance Accountability Act

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Official Development Assistance Accountability Act` to add to the criteria to be met in respect of the provision of official international development assistance abroad by the Government of Canada.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Official Development Assistance Accountability Act

### Section 1

Subsection 4(1) of the `Official Development Assistance Accountability Act` is amended by striking out "and" at the end of paragraph (b) and by adding the following after paragraph (c):

does not promote the interests of a foreign government that has arbitrarily detained a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the `Immigration and Refugee Protection Act` or has engaged in a crime against humanity, genocide or a war crime within the meaning of subsections 6(3) to (5) of the `Crimes Against Humanity and War Crimes Act`; and

is consistent with Canada's efforts to promote international peace and security.
