# Bill S-3: An Act to amend the Offshore Health and Safety Act

**Sponsor**: THE HONOURABLE SENATOR GOLD, P.C.

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Offshore Health and Safety Act` to postpone the repeal of its transitional regulations.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Offshore Health and Safety Act

### Section 1

Subsection 53(5) of the `Offshore Health and Safety Act` is replaced by the following:

**(5)** Unless repealed on an earlier date, the `Canada–Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Marine Installations and Structures Occupational Health and Safety Transitional Regulations`, the `Canada–Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Marine Installations and Structures Transitional Regulations` and the `Canada–Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Area Diving Operations Safety Transitional Regulations` are repealed on the expiry of **eight** years after the day on which this section comes into force.


> **Explanatory Note**:
> Existing text of subsection 53(5):
> *Existing Text*:

### Section 2

Subsection 92(5) of the Act is replaced by the following:

**(5)** Unless repealed on an earlier date, the `Canada–Nova Scotia Offshore Marine Installations and Structures Occupational Health and Safety Transitional Regulations`, the `Canada–Nova Scotia Offshore Marine Installations and Structures Transitional Regulations` and the `Canada–Nova Scotia Offshore Area Diving Operations Safety Transitional Regulations` are repealed on the expiry of **eight** years after the day on which this section comes into force.

## Coordinating Amendments

### Section 3

If this Act receives royal assent during the period beginning on January 1, 2021 and ending on December 31, 2022, then

sections `1` and `2` are deemed not to have come into force and are repealed;

the `Offshore Health and Safety Act` is amended by adding the following after section 53:

### Section 53.1

  **(1)** The `Canada–Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Marine Installations and Structures Occupational Health and Safety Transitional Regulations`, the `Canada–Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Marine Installations and Structures Transitional Regulations` and the `Canada–Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Area Diving Operations Safety Transitional Regulations`, as they read immediately before they were repealed on December 31, 2020, are revived on January 1, 2021.

  **(2)** The regulations that are revived under subsection (1) are repealed on December 31, 2022, unless they are repealed on an earlier date following their revival under this section.

  **(3)** No person shall be convicted of an offence under a provision of a regulation revived under subsection (1) if the offence was committed during the period beginning on January 1, 2021 and ending on the day before the day on which this section comes into force.

the Act is amended by adding the following after section 92:

### Section 92.1

  **(1)** The `Canada–Nova Scotia Offshore Marine Installations and Structures Occupational Health and Safety Transitional Regulations`, the `Canada–Nova Scotia Offshore Marine Installations and Structures Transitional Regulations` and the `Canada–Nova Scotia Offshore Area Diving Operations Safety Transitional Regulations`, as they read immediately before they were repealed on December 31, 2020, are revived on January 1, 2021.

  **(2)** The regulations that are revived under subsection (1) are repealed on December 31, 2022, unless they are repealed on an earlier date following their revival under this section.

  **(3)** No person shall be convicted of an offence under a provision of a regulation revived under subsection (1) if the offence was committed during the period beginning on January 1, 2021 and ending on the day before the day on which this section comes into force.
