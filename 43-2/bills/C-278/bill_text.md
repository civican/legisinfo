# Bill C-278: An Act to amend the Civil Air Navigation Services Commercialization Act

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Civil Air Navigation Services Commercialization Act` to authorize the Minister of Transport to direct NAV CANADA to exclude an airport from any aeronautical study that it conducts or proposes to conduct under the `Canadian Aviation Regulations` in relation to proposed changes to civil air navigation services.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Civil Air Navigation Services Commercialization Act

### Section 1

The `Civil Air Navigation Services Commercialization Act` is amended by adding the following after section 14:

### Section 14.1

The Minister may direct the Corporation to exclude an airport from any aeronautical study it conducts or proposes to conduct under the regulations in relation to proposed changes to civil air navigation services.
