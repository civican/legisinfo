# Bill C-250: An Act to amend the Canada Shipping Act, 2001 (anchorage prohibition)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Shipping Act, 2001` to prohibit the anchoring of vessels within certain waters.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canada Shipping Act, 2001

### Section 1

The `Canada Shipping Act, 2001` is amended by adding the following after section 23:


### Anchorage Prohibition

### Section 23.1

  **(1)** In this section, **prohibited waters** means the waters described in Schedule 1.1.

  **(2)** No person shall anchor a vessel within prohibited waters.

  **(3)** Subsection (2) does not apply in respect of a pleasure craft.

  **(4)** On the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, the Governor in Council may, by order, add a description of prohibited waters to Schedule 1.1.

### Section 2

The Act is amended by adding the following after section 37:

### Section 37.1

Every person who contravenes section 23.1 (anchoring in prohibited waters) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not more than $100,000.

### Section 3

The Act is amended by adding, after Schedule 1, the Schedule 1.1 set out in the schedule to this Act.
