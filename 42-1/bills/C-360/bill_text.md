# Bill C-360: An Act to amend the Navigation Protection Act (certain lakes and rivers in British Columbia)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Navigation Protection Act` in order to add certain lakes and rivers in British Columbia to the navigable waters listed in the schedule to that Act.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

Part 1 of the schedule to the `Navigation Protection Act` is amended by adding after item 10 the following, as indicated:

*Item:*    **10.1**

*Column 1:* **Skaha Lake**

*Column 2:* **49°25′21″ N, 119°35′16″ W**

*Column 3:* **The lake's water boundary includes the mouths of all connecting waterways**

*Item:*    **10.2**

*Column 1:* **Tugulnuit Lake**

*Column 2:* **49°11′55″ N, 119°32′25″ W**
