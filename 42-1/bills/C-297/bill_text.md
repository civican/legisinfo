# Bill C-297: An Act to amend the Canada Elections Act (voting hours)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Elections Act` to change the voting hours across Canada.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

Section 128 of the `Canada Elections Act` is replaced by the following:

### Section 128

The voting hours on polling day are from 7:00 a.m. to **10:00** p.m.

### Section 2

Sections 129 and 131 of the Act are repealed.
