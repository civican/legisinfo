# Bill C-99: An Act to amend the Citizenship Act

**Sponsor**: MINISTER OF IMMIGRATION, REFUGEES AND CITIZENSHIP

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Citizenship Act` to include, in the Oath or Affirmation of Citizenship, a solemn promise to respect the Aboriginal and treaty rights of First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Citizenship Act

### Section 1

The schedule to the `Citizenship Act` is replaced by the schedule set out in the schedule to this Act.


## Coming into Force

### Section 2

This Act comes into force on the day after the day on which it receives royal assent.
