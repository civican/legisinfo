# Bill C-388: An Act to amend the Criminal Code (bestiality)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Criminal Code` to define the term "bestiality".

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Criminal Code

### Section 1

Section 160 of the `Criminal Code` is amended by adding the following after subsection (3):

**(4)** In this section, **bestiality** means any contact by a person, for a sexual purpose, with an animal.
