# Bill C-310: An Act to amend the Financial Administration Act (debt recovery)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Financial Administration Act` to delegate to the Receiver General the authority to recover certain debts due to Her Majesty.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

The `Financial Administration Act` is amended by adding the following after section 155:

### Section 155.01

The Receiver General is authorized to act for the appropriate Minister for the recovery of any prescribed debt that has not been recovered within the prescribed period.
