# Bill C-363: An Act to amend the Species at Risk Act (amendment of the List)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Species at Risk Act` in order to impose obligations on the Minister of the Environment in relation to the amendment of the List of Wildlife Species at Risk.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

  **(1)** Subsection 27(1.1) of the `Species at Risk Act` is replaced by the following:

    **(1.1)** Within one year after receiving an assessment of the status of a species by COSEWIC, the Minister **shall make one of the following recommendations to** the Governor in Council:

      **that** the assessment **be accepted** and the species **be added** to the List;

      **that** the species not **be added** to the List;

      **that** the matter **be referred** back to COSEWIC for further information or consideration.

  **(2)** Subsection 27(3) of the Act is replaced by the following:

    **(3)** **If** the Governor in Council has not taken **any** course of action under subsection (1.1) within **30 days** after **the Minister has made a recommendation**, the Minister shall, by order, amend the List in accordance with COSEWIC's assessment.

### Section 2

The Act is amended by adding the following after section 27:

### Section 27.1

If the Minister does not make an order as required under subsection 27(3), the Minister must prepare a report stating the reasons the order has not been made and indicating when it will be made and cause the report to be laid before each House of Parliament on any of the first 15 days on which that House is sitting after the report is completed.
