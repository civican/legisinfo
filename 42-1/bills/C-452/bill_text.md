# Bill C-452: An Act to amend the Income Tax Act (gift in virtual currency)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Income Tax Act` to provide that a taxpayer's taxable capital gain for a taxation year from the disposition of a property is equal to zero if the disposition is the making of a gift in an eligible virtual currency to a qualified donee.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Income Tax Act

### Section 1

Subparagraph 38(a.1)(i) of the `Income Tax Act` is replaced by the following:

the disposition is the making of a gift to a qualified donee of a share, debt obligation or right listed on a designated stock exchange, a share of the cap­ital stock of a mutual fund corporation, a unit of a mutual fund trust, an interest in a related segrega­ted fund trust (within the meaning assigned by paragraph 138.1(1)(a)), a prescribed debt obligation or **an amount denominated in an eligible virtual currency**,

### Section 2

Subsection 221(1) of the Act is amended by striking out "and" at the end of paragraph (i) and by adding the following after that paragraph:

establishing a schedule that sets out a list of ineligible virtual currencies for the purpose of subparagraph 38(a.1)(i); and
