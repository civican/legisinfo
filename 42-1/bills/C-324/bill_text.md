# Bill C-324: An Act to amend the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act (production of or trafficking in substances)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Controlled Drugs and Substances Act` in order to prohibit the possession, production, sale or importation of anything if it is known that it will be used in the production or traffic of certain substances included in Schedule I to that Act.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

Subsection 7.1(1) of the `Controlled Drugs and Substances Act` is replaced by the following:

### Section 7.1

  **(1)** No person shall possess, produce, sell or import anything knowing that it will be used to produce or traffic in a substance referred to in item **16 or** 18, subitem **19(8)** or **item 25** of Schedule I.
