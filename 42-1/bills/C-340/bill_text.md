# Bill C-340: An Act to amend the Canada Elections Act and to make a consequential amendment to another Act (political financing)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Elections Act` regarding the contribution limits and the computation of the fund paid to registered political parties. It also makes a consequential amendment to the `Income Tax Act` as to the eligible amount of a monetary contribution made to a registered party, a registered association or a candidate.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canada Elections Act

### Section 1

Paragraphs 367(1)(a) to (d) of the `Canada Elections Act` are replaced by the following:

$**500** in total in any calendar year to a particular registered party;

$**500** in total in any calendar year to the registered associations, nomination contestants and candidates of a particular registered party;

$**500** in total **in any calendar year** to a candidate who is not the candidate of a registered party; and

$**1000** in total in any calendar year to the leadership contestants in a particular leadership contest.

### Section 2

Subsection 445(2) of the Act is replaced by the following:

**(2)** An allowance fund for a quarter is the product of

  **$0.4375** multiplied by the number of valid votes cast in the election referred to in subsection (1); and

  **the inflation adjustment factor determined under section 384 that is in effect for that** quarter.
