# Bill C-320: An Act to amend the Canadian Forces Superannu­ation Act and the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act (deletion of deduction from annuity)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canadian Forces Superannuation Act` and the `Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act` to eliminate the deduction of Canada Pension Plan benefits from the annuity payable under each of these Acts.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canadian Forces Superannuation Act

### Section 1

Subsections 15(2), (2.1), (3) and (7) of the `Canadian Forces Superannuation Act` are repealed.

### Section 2

The portion of section 40 of the Act before paragraph (a) is replaced by the following:

### Section 40

  **(1)** If, on the death of a contributor who, on ceasing to be a member of the Canadian Forces, was entitled to an immediate annuity or an annual allowance, there is no person to whom an allowance provided in this Part may be paid, or **if** the persons to whom such **an** allowance may be paid die or cease to be entitled to it and no other amount may be paid to them under this Part, any amount by which the calculated amount, within the meaning of subsection (2), exceeds the aggregate of all amounts paid to those persons and to the contributor under this Part or Part V of the former Act shall be paid

### Section 3

Subparagraph 42(1.1)(a)(i) of the Act is replaced by the following:

four per cent of the portion of his or her salary that is less than or equal to the Year's Maximum Pensionable Earnings, **as determined in accordance with section 18 of the `Canada Pension Plan`**, and

### Section 4

Paragraph 50(1)(k) of the Act is repealed.


## Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act

### Section 5

Subsections 10(2), (2.1), (3) and (7) of the `Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act` are repealed.

### Section 6

Paragraph 26(g) of the Act is repealed.
