# Bill S-210: An Act to amend An Act to amend the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, the Civil Marriage Act and the Criminal Code and to make consequential amendments to other Acts

## Summary

This enactment amends An Act to amend the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, the Civil Marriage Act and the Criminal Code and to make consequential amendments to other Acts by repealing its short title.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

Section 1 of `An Act to amend the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act, the Civil Marriage Act and the Criminal Code and to make consequential amendments to other Acts`, being chapter 29 of the Statutes of Canada, 2015, is repealed.
