# Bill C-43: An Act respecting a payment to be made out of 

**Sponsor**: MINISTER OF FINANCE

His Excellency the Governor General recommends to the House of Commons the appropriation of public revenue under the circumstances, in the manner and for the purposes set out in a measure entitled "*An Act respecting a payment to be made out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund to support a pan-Canadian artificial intelligence strategy*".

## Summary

This enactment authorizes a payment to be made out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund to the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research to support a pan-Canadian artificial intelligence strategy.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Payment to the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research

### Section 1

There may be paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund, on the requisition of the Minister of Industry, a sum not exceeding $125,000,000 to the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research to support a pan-Canadian artificial intelligence strategy.
