# Bill C-321: An Act to amend the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999 (prohibition of asbestos)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999` to prohibit the manufacture, use, sale, offer for sale or import of certain toxic substances that cause significant danger to the environment or to human life or health.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

The `Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999` is amended by adding the following after section 94:


## Prohibited Substances

### Section 94.1

No person shall manufacture, use, sell, offer for sale or import a toxic substance listed in Schedule 2.1 or a product containing it.

### Section 94.2

  **(1)** The Ministers may, by order, add to Schedule 2.1 a substance specified on the List of Toxic Substances in Schedule 1, if they believe that doing so is required to deal with a significant danger to the environment or to human life or health, or delete any substance from Schedule 2.1.

  **(2)** Before a substance is added to or deleted from Schedule 2.1, the Minister shall give the Committee an opportunity to advise the Ministers.

### Section 2

Paragraph 272(1)(c) of the Act is replaced by the following:

contravenes a prohibition imposed under subsection 82(1) or (2), paragraph 84(1)(b), **section 94.1,** subsection 107(1) or (2), paragraph 109(1)(b) or subsection 186(1) or 225(4);

### Section 3

The Act is amended by adding, after Schedule 2, Schedule 2.1 set out in the Schedule to this Act.

### Section 4

This Act comes into force one year after the day on which it receives royal assent.
