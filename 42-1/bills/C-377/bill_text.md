# Bill C-377: An Act to change the name of the electoral district of Châteauguay—Lacolle

## Summary

This enactment changes the name of the electoral district of Châteauguay—Lacolle to "Châteauguay—Les Jardins-de-Napierville".

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

In the representation order declared in force by proclamation of October 1, 2013, under the `Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act`, paragraph 21 of the part relating to Quebec is amended by substituting the name "Châteauguay—Les Jardins-de-Napierville" for the name "Châteauguay—Lacolle".
