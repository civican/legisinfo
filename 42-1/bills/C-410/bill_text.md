# Bill C-410: An Act to amend the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act (property of bankrupt — exclusion)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act` to exclude property in a registered education savings plan and a registered disability savings plan from the property of a bankrupt.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act

### Section 1

Paragraph  67(1)(b.3) of the `Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act` is replaced by the following:

without restricting the generality of paragraph (b), property in a registered retirement savings plan, a registered retirement income fund, a **registered education savings plan or a registered disability savings plan**, as those expressions are defined in the `Income Tax Act`, or in any prescribed plan, other than property contributed to any such plan or fund in the 12 months before the date of bankruptcy,
