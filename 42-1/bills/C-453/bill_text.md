# Bill C-453: An Act to amend the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act (property of bankrupt — registered education savings plan)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act` to exclude property in a registered education savings plan from the property of a bankrupt.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act

### Section 1

Paragraph  67(1)(b.3) of the `Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act` is replaced by the following:

without restricting the generality of paragraph (b), property in a registered retirement savings plan, a registered retirement income fund or a **registered education savings plan**, as those expressions are defined in the `Income Tax Act`, or in any prescribed plan, other than property contributed to any such plan or fund in the 12 months before the date of bankruptcy,


## Coordinating Amendment

### Section 2

If Bill C-97, introduced in the 1st session of the 42nd Parliament and entitled the `Budget Implementation Act, 2019, No. 1`, receives royal assent, then, on the first day on which both section 134 of that Act and section 1 of this Act are in force, paragraph 67(1)(b.3) of the `Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act` is replaced by the following:

without restricting the generality of paragraph (b), property in a registered retirement savings
plan, a registered retirement income fund, a registered education savings plan or a registered disability savings plan, as those expressions are defined in the `Income Tax Act`, or in any prescribed plan, other than property contributed to any such plan or fund in the 12 months before the date of bankruptcy,
