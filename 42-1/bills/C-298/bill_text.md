# Bill C-298: An Act to amend the Canada Elections Act (voting hours — Pacific time zone)

## Summary

This enactment changes the hours of voting in the Pacific time zone on polling day.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

Paragraph 128(1)(d) of the `Canada Elections Act` is replaced by the following:

from **8:00** a.m. to **8:00** p.m., if the electoral district is in the Pacific time zone.
