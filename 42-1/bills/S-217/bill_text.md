# Bill S-217: An Act to amend the Criminal Code (detention in custody)

**Sponsor**: Runciman

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Criminal Code` to

expand the grounds for the justification of detention in custody;

require that, in any proceeding under section 515, the prosecutor lead evidence to prove the fact that the accused has failed to appear in court when required to do so and the fact that the accused has previously been convicted of a criminal offence or has been charged with and is awaiting trial for another criminal offence; and

require that a court, when exercising its discretion to grant credit for time spent in custody in determining the sentence to be imposed on a person convicted of an offence, consider the reasons for detaining the person in custody.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

Paragraph 515(10)(c) of the `Criminal Code` is amended by striking out "and" at the end of subparagraph (iii) and by adding the following after subparagraph (iv):

the fact that the accused has failed to appear in court on one or more occasions when required to do so, and

the fact that the accused has previously been convicted of a criminal offence or has been charged with and is awaiting trial for another criminal offence.


> **Explanatory Note**:
> Existing text of relevant portions of subsection 515(10):
> *Existing Text*:
> > For the purposes of this section, the detention of an accused in custody is justified only on one or more of the following grounds:
> > 
> > if the detention is necessary to maintain confidence in the administration of justice, having regard to all the circumstances, including
> > . . .
> > the circumstances surrounding the commission of the offence, including whether a firearm was used, and
> > the fact that the accused is liable, on conviction, for a potentially lengthy term of imprisonment or, in the case of an offence that involves, or whose subject-matter is, a firearm, a minimum punishment of imprisonment for a term of three years or more.

### Section 2

Paragraph 518(1)(c) of the Act is replaced by the following:

the prosecutor **shall**, in addition to any other relevant evidence, lead evidence

to prove **the fact** that the accused has previously been convicted of a criminal offence,

to prove **the fact** that the accused has been charged with and is awaiting trial for another criminal offence,

to prove **the fact** that the accused has previously committed an offence under section 145,

to show the circumstances of the alleged offence, particularly as they relate to the probability of conviction of the accused**, or**

to prove the fact that the accused has failed to appear in court on one or more occasions when required to do so;


> **Explanatory Note**:
> Existing text of relevant portions of subsection 518(1):
> *Existing Text*:
> > In any proceedings under section 515,
> > 
> > the prosecutor may, in addition to any other relevant evidence, lead evidence
> > to prove that the accused has previously been convicted of a criminal offence,
> > to prove that the accused has been charged with and is awaiting trial for another criminal offence,
> > to prove that the accused has previously committed an offence under section 145, or
> > to show the circumstances of the alleged offence, particularly as they relate to the probability of conviction of the accused;

### Section 3

Section 719 of the Act is amended by adding the following after subsection (3):

**(3.01)** In exercising its discretion under subsection (3), a court shall take into account that the person was detained in custody for the reasons stated in the record referred to in subsection 515(9.1).
