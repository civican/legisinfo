# Bill S-217: An Act to amend the Criminal Code (detention in custody)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Criminal Code` to

expand the grounds for the justification of detention in custody; and

require that, in any proceeding under section 515, the prosecutor lead evidence to prove the fact that the accused has failed to appear in court when required to do so and the fact that the accused has previously been convicted of a criminal offence or has been charged with and is awaiting trial for another criminal offence.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

Paragraph 515(10)(c) of the `Criminal Code` is amended by striking out "and" at the end of subparagraph (iii) and by adding the following after subparagraph (iv):

the fact that the accused has failed to appear in court on one or more occasions when required to do so, and

the fact that the accused has previously been convicted of a criminal offence or has been charged with and is awaiting trial for another criminal offence.

### Section 2

Paragraph 518(1)(c) of the Act is replaced by the following:

the prosecutor shall, in addition to any other relevant evidence, lead evidence

to prove the fact that the accused has previously been convicted of a criminal offence,

to prove the fact that the accused has been charged with and is awaiting trial for another criminal offence,

to prove the fact that the accused has previously committed an offence under section 145,

to show the circumstances of the alleged offence, particularly as they relate to the probability of conviction of the accused, or

to prove the fact that the accused has failed to appear in court on one or more occasions when required to do so;
