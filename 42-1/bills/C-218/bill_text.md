# Bill C-218: An Act to amend the Canada Transportation Act (railway noise and vibration control)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Transportation Act` to control noise and vibrations produced by the construction or operation of railways located in close proximity to  residential, institutional or commercial establishments at certain times or on certain days.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Short Title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the `Railway Noise and Vibration Control Act.`


## Canada Transportation Act

### Section 2

The `Canada Transportation Act` is amended by adding the following after section 95.1:

### Section 95.11

  **(1)** A railway company constructing or operating a railway within 300 metres of any residential, institutional or commercial establishment shall not shunt, couple or decouple cars or leave an engine with its motor idling

    at any time on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday; and

    before 8:00 a.m. and after 10:00 p.m. on any other day.

  **(2)** During a national emergency within the meaning of section 3 of the `Emergencies Act`, the Minister may authorize the activities referred to in subsection (1) to take place for a period determined by the Minister.

### Section 3

Paragraph 95.2(1)(a) of the Act is replaced by the following:

the elements that the Agency will use to determine whether a railway company is complying with section 95.1 **and subsection 95.11(1)**; and

### Section 4

Subsection 95.3(1) of the Act is replaced by the following:

### Section 95.3

  **(1)** On receipt of a complaint made by any person that a railway company is not complying with section 95.1 **or subsection 95.11(1)**, the Agency may order the railway company to undertake any changes in its railway construction or operation that the Agency considers reasonable to ensure compliance with that section **or subsection**.
