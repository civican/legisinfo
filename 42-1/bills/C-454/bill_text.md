# Bill C-454: An Act respecting net-zero greenhouse gas emissions

## Summary

This enactment requires the Government of Canada to ensure that Canada's greenhouse gas emissions are reduced to net zero by 2050.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the `Net-Zero Greenhouse Gas Emissions Act`.

### Section 2

The Government of Canada must ensure that Canada's greenhouse gas emissions are reduced to net zero by 2050.
