# Bill C-365: An Act to amend the Criminal Code (firefighting equipment)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Criminal Code` to establish specific penalties related to the theft of firefighting equipment. It also creates an aggravating circumstance for sentencing if the mischief involves firefighting equipment. Finally, it establishes sentencing objectives in relation to the theft of such equipment.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

The `Criminal Code` is amended by adding the following after section 333.1:

### Section 333.2

Everyone who commits theft is, if the property stolen is firefighting equipment and the theft causes actual danger to life, guilty of an indictable offence and liable to imprisonment for life.

### Section 2

The Act is amended by adding the following after section 430:

### Section 430.1

When a court imposes a sentence for an offence under section 430, it shall consider as an aggravating circumstance the fact that the property in relation to which the mischief occurred is firefighting equipment.

### Section 3

The Act is amended by adding the following after section 718.02:

### Section 718.021

When a court imposes a sentence for an offence under section 322, the court shall, if the theft involves firefighting equipment, give primary consideration to the objectives of denunciation and deterrence of the conduct that forms the basis of the offence.
