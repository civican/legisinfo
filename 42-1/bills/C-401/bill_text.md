# Bill C-401: An Act to amend the Canada Elections Act (voting age)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Elections Act` to lower the voting age from 18 years to 16.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canada Elections Act

### Section 1

Section 3 of the `Canada Elections Act` is replaced by the following:

### Section 3

Every person who is a Canadian citizen and is **16** years of age or older on polling day is qualified as an elector.


## Coordinating Amendment

### Section 2

If Bill C-33, introduced in the 1st session of the 42nd Parliament and entitled `An Act to amend the Canada Elections Act and to make consequential amendments to other Acts`, receives royal assent, then, on the first day on which both section 1 of that Act and section 1 of this Act are in force, the definition **future elector** in subsection 2(1) of the `Canada Elections Act` is replaced by the following:

**future elector** means a Canadian citizen who is 14 years of age or older but under 16 years of age. (**futur électeur**)
