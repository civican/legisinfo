# Bill C-241: An Act to amend the Excise Tax Act (school authorities)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Excise Tax Act` in order to increase the goods and services tax rebate rate for school authorities from 68% to 100%.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

### Section 1

Paragraph (c) of the definition **specified percentage** in subsection 259(1) of the `Excise Tax Act` is replaced by the following:

in the case of a school authority, **100%**,
