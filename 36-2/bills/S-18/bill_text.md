|  | SUMMARY |  |

|  | This enactment prevents the deployment by the Canadian Forces of persons under the age of eighteen years to a theatre of hostilities. |  |
|  | EXPLANATORY NOTE |  |
|  | National Defence Act |  |
|  | Clause 1: New. |  |

|  | 2nd Session, 36th Parliament, 48-49 Elizabeth II, 1999-2000 |
| --- | --- |

The Senate of Canada # BILL S-18 An Act to amend the National Defence Act (non-deployment of persons under the age of eighteen years to theatres of hostilities) R.S., c. N-5 Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: **1. The *National Defence Act* is amended by adding the following after section 33:** *Limitation on Deployment* Persons under eighteen **34.** A person who is under the age of eighteen years may not be deployed by the Canadian Forces to a theatre of hostilities.
