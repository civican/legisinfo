The House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-445 An Act to change the name of the electoral district of Rimouski - Mitis Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: Name changed to ``La Mitis- et-Rimouski- Neigette'' **1.** In the representation order declared in force by Proclamation of January 8, 1996, under the *Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act*, paragraph 56 of that part relating to the province of Quebec is amended by substituting the name ``La Mitis-et-Rimouski-Neigette'' for the name ``Rimouski - Mitis''.
