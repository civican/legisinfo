The House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-360 An Act to amend the Extradition Act (definition of ``child'') Preamble Whereas, on November 20, 1989, the Convention on the Rights of the Child was adopted by the United Nations General Assembly; Whereas the Convention was ratified by Canada on December 31, 1991; And Whereas the Convention proposes a definition of ``child'' that should be applied uniformly throughout federal legislation; 1999, c. 18 Now, Therefore, Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: **1. Item 12 of Schedule I to the *Extradition Act* is replaced by the following:** **12.** Stealing a child under eighteen years of age
