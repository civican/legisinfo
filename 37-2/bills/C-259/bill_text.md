|  | At present the Criminal Code provides that a court must, in imposing a sentence, take into account the principle that imprisonment should, within reason, be seen as a last resort, especially for Aboriginal offenders. With this enactment the court is no longer mandated to give such special consideration to aboriginal offenders. |  |

House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-259 An Act to amend the Criminal Code R.S., c. C-46 Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: **1. Paragraph 718.2(*e*) of the *Criminal Code* is replaced by the following:** (*e*) all available sanctions other than imprisonment that are reasonable in the circumstances should be considered for all offenders.
