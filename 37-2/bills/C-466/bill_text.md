| This enactment expands the scope of section 270.1 of the Criminal Code to provide that anyone who wilfully interferes with the protective equipment of a peace officer while the officer is engaged in the execution of his or her duties is guilty of an offence. | Le texte élargit la portée de l'article 270.1 du Code criminel afin que quiconque dérange volontairement le matériel de protection d'un agent de la paix agissant dans l'exercice de ses fonctions soit coupable d'une infraction. |
