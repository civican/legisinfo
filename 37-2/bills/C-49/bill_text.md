House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-49 An Act respecting the effective date of the representation order of 2003 Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: Effective date of representa- tion order **1.** Despite subsection 25(1) of the *Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act* and the proclamation issued under that subsection on August 25, 2003 and registered as SI/2003-154, the representation order referred to in that proclamation is effective on the first dissolution of Parliament that occurs on or after April 1, 2004.
