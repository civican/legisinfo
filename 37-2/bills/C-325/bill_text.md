| Volunteer emergency service | 1. Section 60 of the Income Tax Act is amended by striking out the word "and" at the end of paragraph (w), by adding the word "and" at the end of paragraph (x) and by adding the following after paragraph (x): (y) $3,000, where the taxpayer performed at least 200 hours of volunteer service in the taxation year as an ambulance technician, a firefighter or a person who assists in search or rescue operations or in other emergency situations. |  | 1.
