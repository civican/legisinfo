House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-406 An Act to amend the Divorce Act (custody of grandchildren) R.S., c. 3 (2nd Supp.) Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: **1. Subsection 16(3) of the *Divorce Act* is replaced by the following:** Application by other person (3) A person, other than a spouse or a grandparent related to the children by consanguinity or adoption , may not make an application under subsection (1) or (2) without leave of the court.
