House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-329 An Act to amend the Criminal Code (protection of children) Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: **1. Section 43 of the *Criminal Code* is repealed.**
