House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-259 An Act to amend the National Defence Act (Snowbirds) R.S., 1985, c. N-5 Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: **1. The *National Defence Act* is amended by adding the following after section 17:** Protection of Canada's Military Heritage Snowbirds **17.1** (1) The Minister shall ensure that the Snowbirds Squadron is maintained and that the squadron has sufficient resources to carry out its mission. Definition of ``Snowbirds Squadron'' (2) For the purposes of this section, ``Snowbirds Squadron'' means a squadron commonly referred to as the ``Snowbirds'', belonging to the Royal Canadian Air Force, whose mission is to demonstrate the skill, professionalism and teamwork of the Canadian Forces to the general public.
