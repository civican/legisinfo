| Regulations | 10. (1) The Minister may, on the recommendation of the Committee, make regulations that the Minister considers necessary to give effect to the purposes and provisions of this Act, including regulations respecting (a) the establishment of national standards for safe drinking water; (b) the monitoring of compliance with the National Standards, including the appointment of inspectors and their power to enter into and inspect community water systems, to seize objects, request the production of information and documents and to make copies of them; and (c) the reporting and public disclosure of incidents of non-compliance referred to in section 9, including the information that must be reported and disclosed and the time period and manner in which it must be done. |  | 10.
