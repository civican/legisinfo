# Bill C-235: An Act to amend the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act and to make consequential amendments to other Acts

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Controlled Drugs and Substances Act` to repeal a provision that makes it an offence to possess certain substances.

It also makes consequential amendments to other Acts.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Short Title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the `Ending the Stigma of Substance Use Act`.
