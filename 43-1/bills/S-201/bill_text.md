# Bill S-201: An Act to amend the Borrowing Authority Act

**Sponsor**: Senator Day

## Summary

This enactment amends the`Borrowing Authority Act` to limit the circumstances in which the Governor in Council may authorize the borrowing of money without legislative approval.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Borrowing Authority Act

### Section 1

Section 3 of the `Borrowing Authority Act` is replaced by the following:

### Section 3

The Minister may, under and in accordance with **an Act of Parliament**, borrow money on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada, by way of the issue and sale of securities, as defined in section 2 of **the `Financial Administration Act`**, or otherwise.
