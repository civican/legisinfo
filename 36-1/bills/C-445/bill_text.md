The House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-445 An Act to change the name of the electoral district of Stormont-Dundas Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: Name changed to ``Stormont- Dundas- Charlotten- burgh'' **1.** In the representation order declared in force by Proclamation of January 8, 1996, under the *Electoral Boundaries Readjustment Act*, paragraph 83 of that part relating to the province of Ontario is amended by substituting the name ``Stormont-Dundas-Charlottenburgh'' for the name ``Stormont-Dundas''.
