|  | This enactment amends the exceptions to the accelerated parole review procedure in sections 125 and 126 of the Corrections and Conditional Release Act. The procedure would not be available for |  |
|  | (a) the offences set out in Schedule II of that Act; |  |
|  | (b) the offence provided for in section 463 of the Criminal Code (attempts, accessories) in relation to one of the laundering proceeds of certain offences referred to in paragraphs (1)(e), (2)(f) and (3)(e) of Schedule II of the Corrections and Conditional Release Act. |  |
|  | It should be noted that this enactment broadens the exception for the offences set out in Schedule II of the Corrections and Conditional Release Act in that they no longer have to be offences in respect of which an order has been made under section 743.6 of the Criminal Code. |  |

The House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-416 An Act to amend the Corrections and Conditional Release Act (accelerated parole reviews) 1992, c. 20; 1993, c. 34; 1995, cc. 22, 39, 42; 1996, c. 19; 1997, c. 17 Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: **1. Subparagraph 125(1)(*a*)(iv) of the *Corrections and Conditional Release Act* is replaced by the following:** (iv) an offence set out in Schedule II, or (v) an offence under section 463 of the *Criminal Code* relating to an offence set out in paragraph (1)(*e*), (2)(*f*) or (3)(*e*) of Schedule II that was prosecuted by way of indictment;
