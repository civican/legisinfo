The House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-57 An Act to amend the Bell Canada Act 1987, c. 19; 1991, c. 11; 1992, c. 1; 1993, c. 38 Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: 1991, c. 11, s. 72 **1. Section 7 of the *Bell Canada Act* is repealed.**
