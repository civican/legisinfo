**HOUSE OF COMMONS OF CANADA** # BILL C-244 An Act requiring the national flag of Canada to be flown at half-mast on Remembrance Day First reading, October 23, 2002

|  | SUMMARY |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | The purpose of this enactment is to provide that, on Remembrance Day, the national flag of Canada shall be flown at half-mast on federal premises throughout Canada. |  |

|  | 2nd Session, 37th Parliament, 51 Elizabeth II, 2002 |
| --- | --- |

House of Commons of Canada # BILL C-244 An Act requiring the national flag of Canada to be flown at half-mast on Remembrance Day Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows: #### SHORT TITLE Short title **1.** This Act may be cited as the *Remembrance Day National Flag Act*. #### INTERPRETATION Definition of ``federal premises'' **2.** In this Act, ``federal premises'' means land or buildings owned or occupied by Her Majesty in right of Canada. #### NATIONAL FLAG OF CANADA National flag of Canada at half-mast **3.** On Remembrance Day, the national flag of Canada shall be flown at half-mast for the entire day on federal premises throughout Canada.
