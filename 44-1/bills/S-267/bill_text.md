# Bill S-267: An Act to amend the Criminal Code (aggravating circumstance — evacuation order or emergency)

**Sponsor**: Senator ATAULLAHJAN

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Criminal Code` to provide that taking advantage of the fact that a place is under an evacuation order or is experiencing a natural disaster or emergency is to be considered an aggravating circumstance for sentencing purposes.

His Majesty, by and with the advice
and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of
Canada, enacts as follows:


## `Criminal Code`

### Section 1

Paragraph 718.‍2(a) of the `Criminal Code` is amended by adding the following after subparagraph (iii.‍2):

evidence that the offender, in committing the offence, took advantage of the fact that the place where the offence was committed was under a lawful evacuation order or was experiencing a natural disaster or other emergency,
