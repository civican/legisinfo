# Bill C-307: An Act to amend the Canada Labour Code (menstrual products)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Labour Code` to require federally regulated employers to make free menstrual products available at the work place.

His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canada Labour Code

### Section 1

Subsection 125(1) of the `Canada Labour Code` is amended by adding the following after paragraph (i):

make free menstrual products readily available to persons who are granted access to the work place, in accordance with the regulations;
