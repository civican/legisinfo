# Bill C-287: An Act to amend the Pest Control Products Act (glyphosate)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Pest Control Products Act` to prohibit the manufacturing, possession, handling, storage, transportation, importation, distribution or use of glyphosate.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Pest Control Products Act


### Amendment to the Act

### Section 1

Section 6 of the `Pest Control Products Act` is amended by adding the following after subsection (1):

**(1.1)** No person shall manufacture, possess, handle, store, transport, import, distribute or use glyphosate as a pest control product.


### Transitional Provision

### Section 2

The registration of products containing glyphosate under the `Pest Control Products Act` is cancelled.


## Coming into Force

### Section 3

Section 1 comes into force on the first anniversary of the day on which this Act receives royal assent.
