# Bill C-346: An Act to amend the Canada Shipping Act, 2001 (certificate of competency)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Shipping Act, 2001` in order for a foreign national who holds a diploma issued by a prescribed Canadian maritime institution and for whom a permanent residence application is being processed to be eligible for a certificate of competency.

His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canada Shipping Act, 2001

### Section 1

The definition qualified person in section 2 of the `Canada Shipping Act, 2001` is amended by striking out "or" at the end of paragraph (a) and by adding the following after that paragraph:

a foreign national within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the `Immigration and Refugee Protection Act` who is referred to in subsection 88(1); or

### Section 2

Subsection 88(1) of the Act is replaced by the following:

### Section 88

  **(1)** Only **the following persons** may hold a certificate of competency that is issued under this Part:

    a Canadian citizen;

    a permanent resident within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the `Immigration and Refugee Protection Act`;

    a foreign national within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the `Immigration and Refugee Protection Act` who holds a diploma issued by a prescribed Canadian maritime institution and for whom a permanent residence application under that Act is being processed.

### Section 3

Section 100 of the Act is amended by adding the following after paragraph (c):

designating Canadian maritime institutions for the purpose of subsection 88(1);
