# Bill C-380: An Act to amend the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999 (plastic manufactured items)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999` to delete plastic manufactured items from the list of toxic substances in Schedule 1 to that Act.

His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999

### Section 1

Item 132 of Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the `Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999` is repealed.
