# Bill C-227: An Act to amend the Canada Elections Act (voting age)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Elections Act` to lower the federal voting age in Canada from 18 to 16 years of age.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canada Elections Act

### Section 1

The definition future elector in subsection 2(1) of the `Canada Elections Act` is replaced by the following:

**future elector** means a Canadian citizen who is 14 years of age or older but under **16** years of age. (**futur électeur**)

### Section 2

Section 3 of the Act is replaced by the following:

### Section 3

Every person who is a Canadian citizen and is **16** years of age or older on polling day is qualified as an elector.

### Section 3

Subsection 22(5) of the Act is repealed.

### Section 4

  **(1)** Subparagraph 281.3(a)(ii) of the Act is replaced by the following:

    is not **16** years of age or older — or will not be **16** years of age or older — on polling day; or

  **(2)** Subparagraph 281.3(b)(ii) of the Act is replaced by the following:

    is not **16** years of age or older — or will not be **16** years of age or older — on polling day.

### Section 5

Paragraph 549.1(1)(b) of the Act is replaced by the following:

the elector is **16** years of age or older or will be **16** years of age or older on polling day;
