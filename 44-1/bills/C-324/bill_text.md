# Bill C-324: An Act to amend the Special Economic Measures Act

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Special Economic Measures Act` to change that Act's long title to "`An Act to provide for the imposition of economic measures against a person, an entity or a foreign state for grave breaches of international peace and security, gross and systematic human rights violations or acts of significant corruption`" and its short title to the "`Sergei Magnitsky Global Sanctions Act`".

His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Special Economic Measures Act


### Amendments to the Act

### Section 1

The long title of the `Special Economic Measures Act` is replaced by the following:

An Act to provide for the imposition of economic measures against a person, an entity or a foreign state for grave breaches of international peace and security, gross and systematic human rights violations or acts of significant corruption

### Section 2

Section 1 of the Act is replaced by the following:

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the `**Sergei Magnitsky Global Sanctions Act**`.


### Terminology

### Section 3

Every reference to the "`Special Economic Measures Act`" is replaced by a reference to the "`Sergei Magnitsky Global Sanctions Act`" in the following provisions:

paragraph 13(3)(d) of the `Seized Property Management Act`;

paragraph 11.11(1)(b.1) of the `Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act`;

paragraph 35(1)(d) of the `Immigration and Refugee Protection Act`;

paragraph 6(2)(d) of the `International Interests in Mobile Equipment (aircraft equipment) Act`;

subsection 20(1) of the `Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act`; and

in the `Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act (Sergei Magnitsky Law)`:

the long title,

the preamble, and

subsection 16(1).

### Section 4

Every reference to the "`Special Economic Measures Act`" is replaced by a reference to the "`Sergei Magnitsky Global Sanctions Act`" in the following provisions:

subsection 15(2) of the `Special Economic Measures (Burma) Regulations`;

subsection 10(2) of the `Special Economic Measures (Zimbabwe) Regulations`;

subsection 10(2) of the `Special Economic Measures (Iran) Regulations`;

in the `Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions and Imposing Special Economic Measures on Libya`:

subsection 2(2), and

subsection 24(2);

paragraph 3(1)(c) of the *General Export Permit No. 46 — Cryptography for Use by Certain Consignees*;

subsection 7(2) of the `Special Economic Measures (Russia) Regulations`;

subsection 7(2) of the `Special Economic Measures (Ukraine) Regulations`;

subsection 7(2) of the `Special Economic Measures (South Sudan) Regulations`;

subsection 7(2) of the `Special Economic Measures (People's Republic of China) Regulations`.
