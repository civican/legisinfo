# Bill S-221: An Act to amend the Governor General's Act (retiring annuity and other benefits)

**Sponsor**: Senator Carignan, P.C.

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Governor General's Act` to provide that a Governor General is not eligible for a retiring annuity or other benefits unless they hold office for at least five consecutive years.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:

## Transitional Provision

### Section 4

  **(1)** The payment, under Part II of the `Governor General's Act` as it read immediately before the day on which this Act comes into force, of an annuity to a former Governor General who held office for less than five consecutive years ceases on the day on which this Act comes into force.

  **(2)** The payment, under Part II of the `Governor General's Act` as it read immediately before the day on which this Act comes into force, of an annuity to a survivor shall continue during the life of the survivor.
