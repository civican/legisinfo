# Bill C-405: An Act to amend the Criminal Code and the Parliament of Canada Act

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Criminal Code` to include a minimum punishment of imprisonment for a term of six months for cases of perjury before a House of Parliament. It also amends the `Parliament of Canada Act` to provide that a House of Parliament may impose a fine on a person who engages in conduct that amounts to contempt of Parliament.

His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Criminal Code

### Section 1

Section 132 of the `Criminal Code` is replaced by the following:

### Section 132

Every person who commits perjury is guilty of an indictable offence and liable

if the offence is committed before a House of Parliament or one of its committees, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years and to a minimum punishment of imprisonment for a term of six months; or

in any other case, to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 14 years.


## Parliament of Canada Act

### Section 2

The `Parliament of Canada Act` is amended by adding the following after section 6:


## Fine for Contempt

### Section 6.1

  **(1)** A House of Parliament may, by order, impose a fine of not more than $50,000 on a person who engages in conduct that amounts to contempt of Parliament, as determined by that House.

  **(2)** The order becomes an order of the Federal Court when a certified copy of it is filed in that court, and it may subsequently be enforced as such.

  **(3)** The recovery of the fine is not affected by a prorogation or dissolution of Parliament.

  **(4)** For greater certainty, nothing in this section is to be construed as limiting the privileges, immunities and powers of the Senate or the House of Commons and their members.

### Section 3

Section 12 of the Act is renumbered as subsection 12(1) and is amended by adding the following:

**(2)** For greater certainty, the evidence referred to in subsection (1) is admissible against the witness for the purposes of proceedings under that subsection.
