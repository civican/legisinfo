# Bill C-267: An Act to amend the Excise Act (non-alcoholic beer)


## Summary

This enactment amends the `Excise Act` in order to abolish duties of excise on beer or malt liquor containing not more than 0.5% absolute ethyl alcohol by volume.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Excise Act

### Section 1

  **(1)** The portion of section 3 in Part II of the schedule to the `Excise Act` before paragraph (a) is replaced by the following:

### Section 3

    Per hectolitre of beer or malt liquor containing **more than 0.5% absolute ethyl alcohol by volume but** not more than 1.2% absolute ethyl alcohol by volume,

  **(2)** Part II of the schedule to the Act is amended by adding the following after section 3:

### Section 4

    Per hectolitre of beer or malt liquor containing not more than 0.5% absolute ethyl alcohol by volume, $0.
