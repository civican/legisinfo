# Bill S-222: An Act to amend the Department of Public Works and Government Services Act (use of wood)

**Sponsor**: Senator Griffin

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Department of Public Works and Government Services Act` to permit the Minister, in developing requirements for public works, to allow the use of wood or any other thing that achieves environmental benefits.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Department of Public Works and Government Services Act

### Section 1

Section 7 of the `Department of Public Works and Government Services Act`is amended by adding the following after subsection (1):

**(1.1)** In developing requirements with respect to the construction, maintenance and repair of public works, federal real property and federal immovables, the Minister must consider any potential reduction in greenhouse gas emissions and any other environmental benefits and may allow the use of wood or any other thing — including a material, product or sustainable resource — that achieves such benefits.
