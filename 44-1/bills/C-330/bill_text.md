# Bill C-330: An Act to amend the Canada Labour Code (successor rights and obligations — airports)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Canada Labour Code` to provide that an employer who is an airport authority or a provider of services to an airport authority and who replaces a previous contractor must observe the certification of the previous trade union and the terms and conditions of employment, rights and privileges of the employees of the previous contractor.

His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Canada Labour Code

### Section 1

Section 44 of the `Canada Labour Code` is amended by adding the following after subsection (3):

**(4)** This section applies to an employer who is an airport authority or a provider of services to an airport authority.

### Section 2

Section 47.3 of the Act is amended by adding the following after subsection (2):

**(2.1)** An employer who succeeds a previous contractor and who is an airport authority or a provider of services to an airport authority in accordance with a contract or other arrangement must observe the certification of the previous trade union and the terms and conditions of employment, rights and privileges of the employees of the previous contractor.
