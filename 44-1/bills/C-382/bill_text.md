# Bill C-382: An Act to amend the Excise Tax Act and the Income Tax Act (extra-energy-efficient products)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Excise Tax Act` to exempt extra-energy-efficient products from the goods and services tax. It also amends the `Income Tax Act` to provide a tax credit for the purchase of those products.

Whereas Parliament supports the need to be proactive in reducing energy consumption by encouraging Canadians to use products that are more energy efficient;

And whereas Parliament recognizes the benefits to be gained for all by encouraging a shift towards the use of consumer products that significantly exceed established energy efficiency standards;

Now, therefore, His Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Excise Tax Act

### Section 1

Subsection 123(1) of the `Excise Tax Act` is amended by adding the following in alphabetical order:

**extra-energy-efficient product** means a product that is designated in regulations made under section 125.1; (**produit super-écoénergétique**)

### Section 2

The Act is amended by adding the following after section 125:


#### Regulations

### Section 125.1

  **(1)** For the purposes of this Part in relation to Part XI of Schedule VI, the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister and the Minister of Natural Resources, may make regulations designating certain products or classes of products as extra-energy-efficient products, subject to the following conditions:

    the product or class of products must exceed, by at least 10%, the energy efficiency standard that applies to it under the `Energy Efficiency Regulations, 2016`;

    the product or class of products must not occupy more than 50% of the domestic market share for that product or class of products; and

    consideration must be given to harmonizing, if possible, the basis for the designation with criteria used in other jurisdictions or by relevant organizations to identify products or classes of products whose energy efficiency is exemplary.

  **(2)** Regulations made under subsection (1) must be reviewed regularly, at least once every five years, to ensure that the "extra-energy-efficient" designation reflects technological development and responds to changes in the consumer market and to encourage the use of products whose energy efficiency is exemplary.

### Section 3

Schedule VI to the Act is amended by adding the following after Part X:


## Extra-energy-efficient Products

### Section 1

A supply of the following household appliances that are extra-energy-efficient products:

clothes dryers;

clothes washers;

integrated clothes washer-dryers;

dishwashers;

refrigerators and combination refrigerator-freezers;

freezers;

electric ranges;

dehumidifiers;

microwave ovens;

miscellaneous refrigeration products; and

ceiling fans.

### Section 2

A supply of the following air conditioners, condensing units and chillers that are extra-energy-efficient products:

room air conditioners;

large air conditioners;

packaged terminal air conditioners;

single package central air conditioners;

single package vertical air conditioners;

split system central air conditioners;

large condensing units;

chillers; and

portable air conditioners.

### Section 3

A supply of the following heat pumps that are extra-energy-efficient products:

ground-source heat pumps;

internal water loop heat pumps;

air-source heat pumps;

large heat pumps;

packaged terminal heat pumps;

single package central heat pumps;

single package vertical heat pumps; and

split system central heat pumps.

### Section 4

A supply of the following furnaces and recovery ventilators that are extra-energy-efficient products:

electric furnaces;

recovery ventilators;

energy-recovery ventilators; and

heat-recovery ventilators.

### Section 5

A supply of electric boilers that are extra-energy-efficient products.

### Section 6

A supply of electric water heaters that are extra-energy-efficient products.

### Section 7

A supply of the following lamps and lamp ballasts that are extra-energy-efficient products:

compact fluorescent lamps;

general service lamps;

modified spectrum incandescent lamps;

general service incandescent reflector lamps;

general service fluorescent lamps;

fluorescent lamp ballasts; and

metal halide lamp ballasts.

### Section 8

A supply of the following lighting fixtures that are extra-energy-efficient products:

torchieres; and

ceiling fan light kits.

### Section 9

A supply of the following electronic products that are extra-energy-efficient products:

compact audio products;

video products;

televisions;

external power supplies; and

battery chargers.

### Section 10

A supply of the following motors that are extra-energy-efficient products:

electric motors; and

small electric motors.
