# Bill C-215: An Act to amend the Employment Insurance Act (illness, injury or quarantine)

## Summary

This enactment amends the `Employment Insurance Act` to increase from 15 to 52 the maximum number of weeks for which benefits may be paid because of illness, injury or quarantine.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## Employment Insurance Act

### Section 1

Paragraph 12(3)(c) of the `Employment Insurance Act` is replaced by the following:

because of a prescribed illness, injury or quarantine is **52**;

### Section 2

Paragraph 152.14(1)(c) of the Act is replaced by the following:

because of a prescribed illness, injury or quarantine is **52**;


## Coordinating Amendment

### Section 3

  **(1)** In this section, **other Act** means the `Budget Implementation Act, 2021, No. 1`.

  **(2)** If section 1 of this Act comes into force before subsection 307(2) of the other Act, then that subsection 307(2) and section 323 of the other Act are repealed.

  **(3)** If subsection 307(2) of the other Act comes into force on the same day as section 1 of this Act, then

    that subsection 307(2) is deemed to have come into force before that section 1; and

    section 323 of the other Act is deemed to have come into force before section 2 of this
Act.
