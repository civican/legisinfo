# Bill S-240: An Act to amend the Old Age Security Act (definition of income)

**Sponsor**: Senator QUINN

## Summary

This enactment amends a reference to the *Budget Implementation Act, 2021, No. 1* in the definition of income in the `Old Age Security Act`.

Her Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House of Commons of Canada, enacts as follows:


## `Old Age Security Act`

### Section 1

Subparagraph (c)(i.1) of the
definition income in section 2 of the `Old
Age Security Act` is replaced by the
following:

the amount of the payment under the
program referred to in section **275** of the
`Budget Implementation Act, 2021, No. 1`,


> **Explanatory Note**:
> Relevant portions of the definition:
> *Existing Text*:
> > **income** of a person for a calendar year means the person's income for the year, computed in accordance with the `Income Tax Act`, except that
> > 
> > there shall be deducted from the person's income for the year, to the extent that those amounts have been included in computing that income,
> > 
> > the amount of the payment under the program referred to in section 276 of the `Budget Implementation Act, 2021, No. 1`,
